# Data Analytics Projects & Simulations

This repository contains data analytics projects and business problem-solving tasks completed through virtual academic simulations. The purpose of these projects is to build practical skills in data preparation, spreadsheet manipulation, and interactive dashboard development.

---

 1. Retail Data Visualization & Business Strategy (Tata Simulation)
 **Objective:** Assisting the executive leadership (CEO & CMO) of an online retail store to analyze revenue drivers and identify global expansion territories.
 **Tools Used:** Tableau Public, Data Source Filtering

 **Key Steps Taken:**
  * Cleaned the raw data source by filtering out negative quantities (`Quantity >= 1`) and corrupted zero unit prices (`Unit Price >= 0`).
  * Created a calculated field to accurately measure total business `Revenue`.
  * Developed a monthly Line Chart for 2011 to highlight year-end holiday seasonality trends for operations planning.
  * Built side-by-side Grouped Column Charts and global Choropleth Maps (excluding the UK) to isolate top 10 international markets like Germany, France, and Australia for expansion opportunities.

---

 2. Spreadsheet Management & Modeling (Excel Skills Simulation)
 **Objective:** Learning the core foundations of data processing, operational metrics tracking, and raw data organization.
 **Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel

 **Key Steps Taken:**
  * Cleaned, deduplicated, and formatted raw operational datasets into structured tables.
  * Utilized Excel functions (such as sum, average, max, min and basic logical indexing).
  * Created Pivot Tables and conditional charts to build dynamic metric summaries for managers.

---

 3. Business Context & Requirement Analytics (Deloitte Simulation)
 **Objective:** Analyzing organizational technology requirements and enterprise landscapes to support scaling strategies.
 **Core Competencies:** Requirement Gathering, Process Analysis

 **Key Steps Taken:**
  * Reviewed simulated business cases to extract operational and technical demands.
  * Formulated structured technology roadmaps to align tech architecture with company goals.
